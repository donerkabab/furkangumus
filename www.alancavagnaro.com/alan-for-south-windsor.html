At 19 years old, I ran for and won a seat as a Planning and Zoning Commissioner in my hometown of South Windsor, CT. As an elected commissioner, I have overseen the approval of over 100 development applications since 2021. I helped lead the establishment and unanimous passing of innovative affordable housing zoning regulations and contributed to South Windsor’s 10-year Plan of Conservation and Development. We also passed our new Affordable Housing Plan within the same timeframe. In this role as a commissioner, I analyze existing and proposed regulations to improve community efficiency and livability while advocating for affordable and attainable housing and smart growth policies. I also serve as Vice Chair of the Housing and Fair Rent Commission, where we organize the town’s housing goals and develop strategies for their implementation. The commission includes a judicial role, allowing tenants to report unconscionable rent increases, with our body deciding on potential rent adjustments to ensure fair housing practices.
